Keep or delete

Keep what helps someone make or study education policy. Delete the rest, and keep each thing once.

1The item the card shows. Pick a row, or move with the up and down arrows.

2Type: what the item is. Pick one, then its subtype.

3Keep and next: keeps it and opens the next item. K does the same. The line at the top then says where it waits next.

4Delete: takes it out of the queue. D does the same. Undo is on the line at the top, and on the grayed row at the bottom of the list.

Guess on each real card from the queue, then see its group. About two in five papers, events and opportunities are kept, and one headline in five.

Keep when

  • Tests or changes a policy: research, decisions, wider trends. The research tests a policy, such as teacher pay, attendance or pre-K. A headline reports a decision, a number or a finding, usually from a state or the federal government. A local story shows a wider trend: “Oklahoma School Closures Part of Statewide Picture of Enrollment Decline”.
  • Policy events and money: policy and research conferences, webinars, calls and grants. It is a conference on education policy or research, or its call for proposals. A policy webinar comes from a think tank or research group. It offers education researchers money, a fellowship or a call for proposals, with the deadline ahead.
  • Ours or added by hand: ERC and A&M events, a person's pick. It is an ERC event or an A&M research talk. A person added it: their initials or name follow added by (bulk upload means none were given). Keep it unless it repeats.

Delete when any of these is true

  • Not education policy: off topic, or one campus, district or bill. It is not about education. It is one bill or one rule notice. It is campus news, a scandal or a celebrity. College news that is state policy stays. It is one district's news with no wider point: “Austin ISD faces deeper budget cuts as enrollment declines”. The research has no policy in it: a method, a theory or a program description.
  • Not news we share: opinion, roundups, jobs, awards, teaching or trade conferences. It is opinion, an interview, a podcast, a guide or a roundup. A webinar teaches classroom practice or sells something. It is a job, an award, or a teaching or trade conference. Grants with no deadline or for another field go too.
  • Already in the queue: a second copy of an item. It is a repeat. Keep the best copy.

Finalize

Kept events, ERC events, opportunities, reports and research with no abstract get a description in the ERC's voice. Check each one here before it goes out.

1Needs a rewrite: kept items still waiting for their description.

2Rewrite the descriptions: writes the ones still waiting. The rewrite starts when you keep, so most arrive on their own.

3Original: the text it came with.

4Edit: write or fix the description by hand.

A rewrite that is back waits under To check, its changed words marked.

Check each rewrite

  1. Press Keep, or Use original for the item's own description.
  2. Fix a word with Edit. Saving the edit counts as checked.
  3. Keep the rest (N) keeps every rewrite still to check.

Fix an item later

Fix an item where it is now.

!Delete is final once you reload or close the desk. To bring an item back, add its link again on Submit content.

!Never fix an item in the Sheet's first tab. It is a copy: the desk never reads it and writes over it.

Where it is now

  1. On Content Sort: type over the title, or press Edit under the description; each saves when you click away. For the link, press Change, then Save.
  2. Kept, and not yet in an issue or live: Edit on Finalize, then Save.
  3. On Next issue: Edit on its row.
  4. Waiting on Publish: click the row, then Edit.

Edit on Next issue, Finalize and Publish opens this form.

1Type and Subtype: what the item is, open to change even after Keep. Fix an item kept under the wrong type here, without deleting it.

2Add media: a picture or a PDF flyer.

3Save: keeps the changes. Cancel drops them.
