ERC Content Desk / How-tos

How it all connects

Three steps. Open a box for the whole step.

1 In

Submit content

A link, or a spreadsheet of them. Everything lands in the Content queue.

The whole step
  1. Paste a link and press Add to the queue. Or drop a spreadsheet or doc on the bulk door and press Add all to the queue.
  2. Mondays at 7:00 the scraper files what it found in Drive. Drop the files you want on the bulk door. Run the scraper, on the Content queue, runs it any time.
  3. The public form's items arrive tagged From outside.

2 Decide

Content Sort

Keep, skip or delete. Then check the rewrites on Finalize.

The whole step
  1. Sign in with the desk password.
  2. Give each item a type. Press Keep and next, Skip for now or Delete.
  3. Tick ERC Newsletter only to keep an item off the Exchange. Campus events come ticked.
  4. Open Finalize, the tab beside Sort. Events, opportunities and reports get a rewrite. Press Keep or Use original before you add or publish them.

3 Out

Newsletter

Every kept item can go in.

The whole step
  1. On Next issue, press Add on the items you want. Aim for 3 to 5 research items and 3 to 5 headlines.
  2. Press Open the builder: Outline, Preview & Tweak, Save & Export.
  3. Copy HTML into Outlook on the web and send from erc@tamu.edu.
  4. Press Save to the archive.

How to build the newsletter

3 Out

Policy Exchange

Publish. Skips anything ticked ERC Newsletter only.

The whole step
  1. Open Policy Exchange, then Publish.
  2. Press Publish N to the Exchange, type the desk password, press Confirm. A copy of the list lands in Drive.
  3. Highlight picks up to 6 items for the home page. Each needs a photo.
