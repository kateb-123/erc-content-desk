How to make a research brief

From the paper to the author, in the desk's seven stages.

What you need

  • The desk password. The handoff document says who holds it.
  • Google Drive for desktop, with the shared drive in Finder. Each brief gets a folder in ERC Content Desk/ERC Research Briefs/.
  • Claude Code, opened on that ERC Research Briefs folder. In each prompt below, put your brief's folder in place of holzman-2026.
  • Node.js, Grammarly, Word, Outlook and Chrome.

The house rules, such as VOICE.md and REPORTING.md, sit beside the brief folders in erc-research-briefs/. A brief already with its author picks up at step 5.

1. Paper and notes

  1. Open Research briefs, sign in with the desk password and press New brief.
  2. The desk fills Author's last name from the first author. Change it to the Texas A&M author when that is someone else.
  3. Always add a Topic word. Authors repeat, and the desk cannot rename the folder later.
  4. Put your own name in Brief author. This browser remembers it.

If a step fails with a Drive or script message, see Bring back the Google scripts.

2. Draft

  1. Press Write the draft. If a step still fails after Try again, write to the desk's owner. The handoff document has the address.
  2. If the word pill says short, add interpretation to a thin section, never a restatement.

From here the folder's brief-draft.md is the brief, not the page. Every edit goes in the file. Redraft writes over it, so use it before you edit, never after.

3. Your proof

The first time, make the Grammarly voice from grammarly-voice-setup.md in erc-research-briefs/.

  1. Open brief-for-grammarly.md in the folder and paste all of it into Grammarly. Choose ERC Research Brief Voice and run the Humanizer. The setup file lists which changes to refuse.
  2. Settle each flag in check-notes.md. Keep a number as the paper prints it on the page named. Any you cannot settle become questions for the author.
  3. Check the Brief table or chart against the Paper one beside it. Every number must match at the paper's precision. REPORTING.md has the rest. Table fixes go in proposed-visual.md, in the folder or its figures/.
  4. Paste your proofed text into Claude Code after this prompt:

    Put my proofed text below into holzman-2026/brief-draft.md word for word. Keep its byline, headings, bold, its [Table 1 here] or [Figure 1 here] line and references. List every change to a number, a hedge or a causal verb.

  5. Check each listed change against the paper.

4. With author

The desk stops at Your proof. From here the folder is the record.

  1. Ask Claude Code: Run build-author-docx.js from erc-research-briefs on the holzman-2026 folder. If it says docx is missing, ask it to run npm install docx@9.7.1.
  2. It writes <folder>-author-review.docx into the brief's folder. Any gap shows in red as "Fix before sending: ..." on the page and in the terminal. Never send a copy with a red line.
  3. Email the Word copy to the author of record with a reply date. Add any number questions from step 3.

5. Approved

  1. Save the author's reply in the brief's folder under a new name, like holzman-2026-author-edits.docx. Under the old name, a rebuild would overwrite it. Never edit their file.
  2. Ask Claude Code:

    Number every tracked change and comment in holzman-2026/holzman-2026-author-edits.docx. Mark each Accept or Push back, with the rule from erc-research-briefs/VOICE.md or REPORTING.md. Change no file yet.

  3. Decide each one yourself. Accept facts, corrected numbers, added hedges and plainer wording. Push back on claims firmer than the paper, jargon, "we" and a dropped null result.
  4. Tell Claude which numbered changes to apply. Then reread brief-draft.md for broken sentences.
  5. Reply to the author with a one-line reason for each change you did not make.
  6. Send a second round only if a finding, a number or the table moved. Rebuild the Word copy as in step 4.
  7. Approved means the author's yes in writing. Save that email in the folder as approved.pdf. In Outlook, choose File, Print, then PDF, Save as PDF. From then on the words are frozen.

6. HTML

Start only after the approval. Page breaks depend on the final words.

  1. Ask Claude Code:

    Build holzman-2026/brief.html from a copy of erc-research-briefs/_template/brief-template.html. Never edit the template. Follow STANDARDIZATION.md and REPORTING.md in erc-research-briefs, with erc-research-briefs/bowen-2026/brief.html as the example. The visual is proposed-visual.md. Never cut or reword brief-draft.md to fit: give me options. Build a page at a time and say how far each runs over.

  2. Open brief.html in Chrome and press Cmd+P. Choose Save as PDF. Under More settings, choose Letter and Margins None, tick Background graphics and untick Headers and footers. Print while online so the fonts load.
  3. Send Claude one problem per message, then print again.
  • A page cut off at the bottom, or white space under it: ask for the options. Words move between pages and are never cut.
  • Em dashes: press Cmd+F and type Option+Shift+hyphen. Search for "[" too, for unfilled placeholders.
  • A broken logo: its path should start ../erc-research-briefs/assets/.

7. Live

  1. Save the PDF in the brief's folder, named like Economics-of-Encouragement-ERC-RB-26.pdf.
  2. Open Drive in Chrome and share the PDF as Anyone with the link, Viewer. Copy the link.
  3. Add a row to ERC-Research-Briefs-Library-Metadata.xlsx in erc-research-briefs/, copying the rows above. Ask at the ERC who at the library takes it.
  4. On Submit content, paste the link and pick New research, then ERC Research. Type the brief's title in Title and the Executive Summary's first paragraph in Description. Press Add to the queue.
  5. On Content Sort, fill any blank Date, Authors, Source or Topic. Leave ERC Newsletter only unticked and press Keep and next. How to sort covers Finalize and Publish.
  6. On Newsletter's Next issue, find the brief under Ready to add and press Add.
  7. Email the author of record the public link, with thanks.
