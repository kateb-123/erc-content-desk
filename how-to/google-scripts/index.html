Bring back the Google scripts

For Kathy, or whoever she passes it to. Two scripts, about 15 minutes, any time.

When to do this: once, after Kate's TAMU account closes, or any time sign-ups or the queue's Sheet copy stop updating. It takes about 15 minutes. If you would rather hand it off, send this page to anyone at the ERC with edit access to the ERC Content Desk shared drive folder.

Why

Two small Google scripts run inside two Sheets in the shared drive. They were set up under Kate's TAMU login, so they stop when her account closes. Redeploying them under your own login brings them back. Nothing else changes, and nothing is lost: the desk keeps its own copy of everything.

ScriptWhere it livesWhat stops without it
The queue scriptERC Content Desk, Content queue, the Sheet erc-content-deskThe Sheet copy of the queue, the scraper's files in Drive, the research briefs' folders
The listserv scriptERC Content Desk, Listserv, the Sheet subscribe to listserveThe Join listserv form stops recording sign-ups

Steps, for each of the two Sheets

  1. Open the Sheet in Google Drive. In its menu, choose Extensions, then Apps Script. The script opens in a new tab.
  2. At the top right, click Deploy, then New deployment.
  3. Next to "Select type", click the gear and choose Web app.
  4. Set Execute as to Me (your own address) and Who has access to Anyone. Click Deploy.
  5. Google asks you to authorize. Choose your account; if it warns that the app is unverified, click Advanced, then Go to the project, then Allow. This is our own script.
  6. Copy the Web app URL, the long address that ends in /exec.
  7. Email it to Kate at the address in the handoff document, and say which Sheet it came from (the queue or the listserv). She pastes it into the desk's settings; you do not need a Vercel login.

How to tell it worked

  • The queue script: submit a test item on the team's form, then open the queue Sheet. A new row appears within a minute. Delete the test item on Content Sort afterwards.
  • The listserv script: open the Listserv page on the desk. "Waiting to be added" shows a number, not a warning.

If something goes wrong

  • "Unknown action" or a 500 error on the desk: the address pasted into the desk is from an old deployment. Send Kate the newest Web app URL again.
  • The script asks for a "token" or a folder: do nothing; those settings stay with the script (Project settings, Script properties). Do not delete them.
  • You do not see Extensions, Apps Script: you need edit access to the Sheet. Ask whoever manages the shared drive.
