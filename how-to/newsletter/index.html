Where items come from

The desk's path, one stop at a time. The builder only pulls what reached Next issue.

  1. Submit content, the team form or the public page, puts a link in the queue.
  2. Sort gives each item a type and keeps, skips or deletes it.
  3. Finalize rewrites and checks the description; Publish puts it on the Policy Exchange.
  4. Next issue holds the kept items under Ready to add. Add puts one in the issue; Quick add puts in something new.
  5. The builder pulls what is in the issue, again any time, only new items coming in. An item added by hand in the builder goes to the desk's queue too.

1 Outline

Pick the issue, pull what the desk staged, and put it in order.

  1. Open the builder from Next issue.
  2. Pick the issue, then Pull from the desk. Pull again any time; only new items come in.
  3. To practice, pick a sample under Samples in the Issue list. A sample never saves to the archive.
  4. Drag a section or an item by its grip to move it.
  5. Remove takes an item out. Undo puts it back.

Every control on this step is under The options.

2 Preview & Tweak

Check the issue and tweak anything in place.

  1. Click anything on the email and its card opens: how it is laid out, then its words. Every edit is live; Done closes the card.
  2. Drag an item or a section by the grip at its left to move it on the email.
  3. Add an item and Add a callout sit over the email, with the contents strip.
  4. Every fix here, never in Outlook, so the archive matches what went out.

Every control on this step is under The options.

3 Save & Export

Copy the issue into Outlook, then archive it.

  1. Copy HTML. The next part takes it into Outlook and out from erc@tamu.edu.
  2. Save to the archive. It shows under Past issues.
  3. Start the next issue clears the way for the next one.

Every control on this step is under The options.

Send from erc@tamu.edu

The ERC mailbox can't install add-ins, and HTML pasted into a message as text never formats. So the add-in runs in your personal TAMU mailbox, and the message moves over.

Outlook on the web

  1. On Save & Export, Copy HTML.
  2. In Outlook on the web, in your personal TAMU account, start a new message.
  3. In the message toolbar, open Apps (or More apps), then Insert HTML by Designmodo. The first time: Get Add-ins, search Designmodo, Add.
  4. Paste HTML, paste what you copied, then Insert HTML. The newsletter appears in the message.
  5. Get it to erc@tamu.edu one of two ways. Forward: send the message to erc@tamu.edu, open it there, and forward it on, deleting the forwarded lines at the top. Copy: select all in the composed message, copy, and paste into a new message from erc@tamu.edu.
  6. Address it and send it from erc@tamu.edu.

Never paste the HTML into a message as text, and never copy the .html from a browser window: both come out in the wrong fonts.

The options

Every control, step by step, in the builder's own words.

Outline

  • Issue: the desk's upcoming dates; under Samples, October 6, 2026, as sent, and Sample issue (fictional).
  • Pull from the desk: brings in what Next issue staged. Again any time; only new items come in.
  • The grip on a section or an item: drag it to move it, or press the up and down arrow keys.
  • Move to…: files an item under another section or group.
  • Remove: takes an item out; Undo on its row puts it back until you leave the step.
  • A callout's row: Move to… (after which section) and Remove.
  • Recently discarded: Restore brings back a draft the desk kept.

Preview & Tweak

  • Click anything on the email: its card opens, the introduction, an item or a callout.
  • The grip at the left of an item or a section heading: drag it to move it on the email.
  • The card: Done closes it (every edit is live), Remove, Use original, Undo changes.
  • How it is laid out: Title and details, With description, Stamp beside the text, Headshot beside it all, and for an event the Date card.
  • Picture size, px: a few widths, once a picture layout is on.
  • Zoom available and Flyer link: under the Date card.
  • Kind, for research: Research Brief, Research Report, Journal Article, ERC Explains.
  • Its words: Title, Link, Description with bold, italic and a link, Date, Time, Location or Details, Authors, Media.
  • A callout's card: Its style, Maroon block, Light gray box or Dotted rule, then its words.
  • Over the email: Add an item, Add a callout, Contents strip, Reset layout.

Save & Export

  • Copy HTML: the issue, ready for the add-in.
  • Save to the archive: asks once before replacing an earlier save; off on a sample.
  • Download .html: the same file, saved.
  • Start the next issue: clears the draft and returns to Outline.
