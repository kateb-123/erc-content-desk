1Step 1: Outline

On Next issue, Open the builder. Pick the issue's date: everything staged for it comes in, and anything new each time it opens.

Where items come from

  1. Submit content: anyone on the team sends a link; it waits in the queue.
  2. Sort: each link gets a type and is kept, skipped or deleted.
  3. Finalize and Publish: the description is checked and the item goes on the Policy Exchange.
  4. Next issue: kept items wait under Ready to add. Add puts one in the issue; Quick add types in something new.
  5. The builder: picking the issue brings in what is in it.

Put the issue in the order it should print. Drag a section or an item by its grip; it lands where you drop it.

2Step 2: Preview & Tweak

The email as it will land in Outlook. Click anything on it to change it; what is not on it yet comes from the buttons above.

Click an item and its card opens beside it. Every change shows on the email as you make it; Done closes the card.

Reorder here too: point at an item or a section heading and drag it by the grip that appears at its left.

Fix things here, never in Outlook, so the archive matches what went out.

3Step 3: Save & Export

Copy HTML and send it (the next part). Once it has gone out, Save to the archive, so Past issues keeps it.

Start the next issue clears the draft for the next one.

Send from erc@tamu.edu

The ERC mailbox can't install add-ins, and HTML pasted into a message as text never formats. So the newsletter goes into a message in your personal TAMU account through an add-in, and moves over to erc@tamu.edu from there.

First time only: install the add-in

  1. In Outlook on the web, in your personal TAMU account, open a new message.
  2. Apps in the toolbar, or More apps, then Get Add-ins.
  3. Search Designmodo and add Insert HTML by Designmodo.

The add-in on AppSource Microsoft: how to use add-ins in Outlook

Every issue: a new message in your personal account, and the add-in puts the newsletter in it.

Never paste the HTML into the message as text: it won't format.

Never open the .html in a browser and copy from there: wrong fonts.

Put the newsletter in a message

  1. Copy HTML on Save & Export.
  2. In Outlook on the web, open a new message. Apps in the toolbar, or More apps, then Insert HTML by Designmodo.
  3. Paste into the add-in's Paste HTML box, then Insert HTML. The newsletter appears in the message.

The newsletter is now in a message in your account. Get it into one from erc@tamu.edu, either way, then send.

Forward

  1. Send the message to erc@tamu.edu.
  2. Open it there and forward it to the list.
  3. Delete the forwarded-message lines at the top, then Send.

Or copy

  1. In the message, select all and copy.
  2. Paste it into a new message from erc@tamu.edu, then Send.

How an item can look

Click the item on the email, then pick one under How it is laid out on its card.

Title and details

A plain listing: the title and its details, nothing under them.

With description

When a sentence or two helps: the description prints under the details.

Stamp beside the text

A small photo under the title, beside the description: 96 wide unless you size it.

Headshot beside it all

A portrait beside the whole item, 160 wide: for a speaker.

Date card

For a big ERC event, in ERC Spotlight or Upcoming Events. The block takes the item's Date, Time and Location; under the tile, tick Zoom available and paste a Flyer link. A card never shows a photo.

Research boxes

Every ERC Research item is a tinted box. Kind on its card sets the label at the top; the title is the link, so there is no button.

Research Brief

The kind a pulled item starts as.

Research Report

Give it a chart or photo: Stamp beside the text puts it at the right, 160 wide.

Journal Article

A published paper; name the journal in the description.

ERC Explains

A short explainer in the ERC's own words.

Callouts

Add a callout over the email, then pick Its style on its card. Share something with the ERC always sits last.

Light gray box

Sits inside the section before it: a quiet ask.

Dotted rule

Also inside the section; the Deadline line is optional.

Maroon block

Its own maroon panel, the loudest of the three.

Bold, italic and links

In Description on the card, select the words, then press B, I or the link button.

  • BBold: Dr. Ada ExampleBold for names: speakers, moderators.
  • IItalic: The Imaginary ClassroomItalic for book and report titles.
  • Link: RSVP hereA link prints maroon, bold and underlined.

Photos

Add media on the card. Once Stamp or Headshot is on, Picture size, px appears under the tiles.

Where photos go

Yes: Any item with a description in ERC Research, ERC Spotlight, Upcoming Events or Opportunities.

No: Nothing in Policy Research, Headlines or Miscellaneous: those are lists.

The file

Under Media on the card, Add media and pick the file.

Any size works: the builder shrinks big ones. A GIF goes up as it is, so keep it under 2.5 MB. For a sharp picture, use one at least twice the width it shows: 320 wide for a 160 headshot.

A photo, not a flyer

A flyer is a page of small text, so it reads badly as a picture. Link it instead: Flyer link on a Date card, or a link in the description.

Lists

No descriptions or photos here: linked titles under their group labels, then View more » to the Policy Exchange.

Opportunities

The top of the email

Contents strip

The row of section names under the masthead, each a link into the email. On or off with the Contents strip box over the email.
