# How to sort
https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/sort/

ERC Content Desk (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/) / How-tos (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/)

## How to sort

What to keep, where it goes, and publishing.

## The rule of thumb

Keep what helps someone make or study education policy. Delete the rest. Keep each thing once. About two in five papers, events and opportunities are kept, and one headline in five.

An issue wants 3 to 5 research items or reports from outside and 3 to 5 headlines, all policy relevant.

## Keep when

- The research tests a policy, such as teacher pay, attendance or pre-K.

- A headline reports a decision, a number or a finding, usually from a state or the federal government.

- A local story shows a wider trend. "Oklahoma School Closures Part of Statewide Picture of Enrollment Decline"

- A policy webinar comes from a think tank or research group.

- It is an ERC event or an A&M research talk.

- It offers education researchers money, a fellowship or a call for proposals, with the deadline ahead.

- A person added it. The card shows their initials after added by. Keep it unless it repeats. Bulk uploads show bulk upload there only if Your initials was blank.

Then pick the type and subtype. Item types (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/submit-content/#types) says which.

## Delete when

- It is not about education.

- It is one bill or one rule notice.

- It is campus news, a scandal or a celebrity. College news that is state policy stays.

- It is one district's news with no wider point. "Austin ISD faces deeper budget cuts as enrollment declines"

- It is opinion, an interview, a podcast, a guide or a roundup.

- A webinar teaches classroom practice or sells something.

- It is a job, an award or a conference listing. Grants with no deadline or for another field go too.

- The research has no policy in it. A method, a theory or a program description goes.

- It is a repeat. Keep the best copy. Not every repeat shows Possible duplicate.

## Sources with a clear rule

Source | Kept | What to do | 

ECS State Policy Tracker | 0 of 54 | Delete. Each is one state bill. | 

Austin American-Statesman | 0 of 24 | Delete. Austin ISD and UT campus news. | 

Urban Institute | 6 of 41 | Delete unless it is about schools. | 

NBER | 3 of 18 | Delete unless it is about schooling. | 

K-12 Dive | 6 of 17 | Keep findings and state counts. Delete Week In Review. | 

EdWorkingPapers | 32 of 58 | Keep most. Delete methods papers. | 

Kept nearly every time: CALDER, Texas Tribune, the Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board, the Tennessee Education Research Alliance, and ERC and A&M events.

Deleted nearly every time: Texas Public Radio, KUT, PBS NewsHour, the Department of Education newsroom, the Federal Register, WestEd and AIR.

## ERC Newsletter only

A kept item goes to the newsletter and the Policy Exchange. Tick ERC Newsletter only to keep it off the Exchange.

- Campus events from other units stay ticked. The desk ticks any Event with the A&M subtype.

- Tick it for a notice meant for a small campus audience.

- Untick it for an A&M talk by an education policy researcher.

- Give ERC events the ERC event type. Then they go everywhere.

## Not sure

Press Skip for now. The item drops below the waiting items with a Skipped tag until you decide.

## Past and Already live

Past holds events and opportunities whose date or deadline has passed. Already live holds links the Exchange already has. Press Dismiss all on each group.

## Publish to the Exchange

Check the rewrites first. Events, ERC events, opportunities, reports and research with no abstract get a description in the ERC's voice. On the Finalize tab, press Keep or Use original on each.

- Open Publish from the Policy Exchange page.

- Press Publish N to the Exchange. Type the desk password, the one Content Sort asks for. Press Confirm.

No password? Ask the desk's owner. The handoff document has their contact.

Each publish files the site's whole list in Drive, under ERC Content Desk, Policy Exchange. Publishing only adds rows. An edit after an item is live never reaches the site.

Highlight, under the table, sets the items on the Exchange's home page.

## Send from erc@tamu.edu

The ERC mailbox can't install add-ins, and HTML pasted into a message as text never formats. So the newsletter goes into a message in your personal TAMU account through an add-in, and moves over to erc@tamu.edu from there.

## First time only: install the add-in

- In Outlook on the web, in your personal TAMU account, open a new message.
 
- Apps in the toolbar, or More apps, then Get Add-ins.
 
- Search Designmodo and add Insert HTML by Designmodo.

 The add-in on AppSource (https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-us/product/office/wa200002918)
 Microsoft: how to use add-ins in Outlook (https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook/getstarted/use-add-ins-in-outlook)

Every issue: a new message in your personal account, and the add-in puts the newsletter in it.
 
Never paste the HTML into the message as text: it won't format.
 
Never open the .html in a browser and copy from there: wrong fonts.

## Put the newsletter in a message

- Copy HTML on Save & Export.
 
- In Outlook on the web, open a new message. Apps in the toolbar, or More apps, then Insert HTML by Designmodo.
 
- Paste into the add-in's Paste HTML box, then Insert HTML. The newsletter appears in the message.

The newsletter is now in a message in your account. Get it into one from erc@tamu.edu, either way, then send.

## How to change the Policy Exchange

Add, fix, remove, highlight, and the look.

## What the site is

Two public files in its GitHub repo: data/news.csv, one line per item, and data/featured.json, what the New & Upcoming card shows and what is hidden.

The desk's Publish adds lines to the first and sets the card's pins in the second. The site's edit door changes both.

## Add items

- On Content Sort, leave ERC Newsletter only unticked. Ticked, the item never reaches the site; an A&M event comes ticked. Press Keep and next.

- Open Policy Exchange, then Publish.

- Press Publish N to the Exchange, type the desk password, press Confirm.

## Fix a live item

Publish only adds lines, so a desk edit to a live item stays on the desk. Fix it on the edit door, on a laptop:

- On the Policy Exchange hub, open Edit live items. Sign in with the Exchange's own password, not the desk's.

- Press Edit on the item, fix it, press Save.

## Remove a live item

The desk's Delete never touches the site. On the edit door, press Edit, then:

- Hide from the site: off the pages, still in the public news.csv. Show again is under Hidden items in the top strip.

- Delete…, then Delete it: the line goes. Undo lasts only while its note shows.

Past events, closed opportunities and headlines over 60 days old leave by themselves.

## Change the highlight

The big card on the home page has its own page: Policy Exchange, then Highlight. It works any day, with or without anything to publish.

- In Pick from, press Highlight on a row. Set its photo and its Until day.

- Press Update the highlight, type the desk password, press Confirm.

Publishing never changes the card. An event or opportunity pick gets card words; the card shows them, and the photo, in place of the item's own.

A Drive picture link never shows on the card; use one ending .jpg, .png or .webp.

An announcement card is Add to New & Upcoming, in the edit door's top strip.

## Change a logo or the look

## How to change the desk

What you can change from the screens, and what needs code.

## From the screens

Content Sort and Research briefs ask for the desk password. Publish and Highlight ask for it at Confirm.

An item

Fix it where it is now.

- On Content Sort: type over the title, or press Edit under the description; each saves when you click away. For the link, press Change, then Save. Pick a type for its other fields.

- Kept: Edit on Finalize, then Save. Finalize drops it once it is in an issue or live.

- On Next issue: Edit on its row. Already in the builder? Fix it on Preview & Tweak too.

- Waiting on Publish: click the row, then Edit.

That Edit also changes the type, the subtype and ERC Newsletter only.

Add media adds a picture or PDF flyer. Keep a GIF under 2.5 MB.

Delete is final once you leave the page. To bring an item back, add its link again on Submit content.

Never fix an item in the Sheet's first tab. It is a copy: the desk never reads it and writes over it.

An issue

- To fill a later issue, press its date beside Later under This issue.

- Build each issue on one computer: the draft lives in that browser.

- Edits on Preview & Tweak (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/newsletter/#tweak) change the email, not the desk.

- To fix an archived issue, fix it in the builder on the same computer and press Save to the archive again. This works only until you press Start the next issue.

Send dates

- On Newsletter's Schedule tab, open the Sheet from its link, then its schedule tab.

- Type each date in column A, from row 2, as 2026-11-10. A date shown as 11/10/2026 is skipped.

The desk shows it within ten minutes. If not, see Bring back the Google scripts (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/google-scripts/).

The Exchange

A desk edit never reaches a live item. To fix one:

- On Policy Exchange, open Edit live items on a laptop. It does not load on a phone.

- Find the item on the site, press Edit, fix it, press Save.

In the same Edit, Hide from the site takes it down; the desk's Delete never touches the site.

The highlight (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/exchange/#highlight) card never shows a Drive picture. Use Add photo on Highlight, or a link ending .jpg, .png or .webp.

The listserv

The count is Sheet 1 as it was when the desk loaded. Reload the browser page to update it.

The scraper

Rows you delete on the Newsletter scrapes tabs stay deleted. The scraper does not read the Scraper sources Sheet; changing its sources needs code.

# How to make a research brief
https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/research-briefs/

ERC Content Desk (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/) / How-tos (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/)

## How to make a research brief

From the paper to the author, in the desk's seven stages.

## What you need

- The desk password. The handoff document says who holds it.

- Google Drive for desktop, with the shared drive in Finder. Each brief gets a folder in ERC Content Desk/ERC Research Briefs/.

- Claude Code, opened on that ERC Research Briefs folder. In each prompt below, put your brief's folder in place of holzman-2026.

- Node.js, Grammarly, Word, Outlook and Chrome.

The house rules, such as VOICE.md and REPORTING.md, sit beside the brief folders in erc-research-briefs/. A brief already with its author picks up at step 5.

## 1. Paper and notes

- Open Research briefs (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/#briefs), sign in with the desk password and press New brief.

- The desk fills Author's last name from the first author. Change it to the Texas A&M author when that is someone else.

- Always add a Topic word. Authors repeat, and the desk cannot rename the folder later.

- Put your own name in Brief author. This browser remembers it.

If a step fails with a Drive or script message, see Bring back the Google scripts (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/how-to/google-scripts/).

## 2. Draft

- Press Write the draft. If a step still fails after Try again, write to the desk's owner. The handoff document has the address.

- If the word pill says short, add interpretation to a thin section, never a restatement.

From here the folder's brief-draft.md is the brief, not the page. Every edit goes in the file. Redraft writes over it, so use it before you edit, never after.

## 3. Your proof

The first time, make the Grammarly voice from grammarly-voice-setup.md in erc-research-briefs/.

- Open brief-for-grammarly.md in the folder and paste all of it into Grammarly. Choose ERC Research Brief Voice and run the Humanizer. The setup file lists which changes to refuse.

- Settle each flag in check-notes.md. Keep a number as the paper prints it on the page named. Any you cannot settle become questions for the author.

- Check the Brief table or chart against the Paper one beside it. Every number must match at the paper's precision. REPORTING.md has the rest. Table fixes go in proposed-visual.md, in the folder or its figures/.

- Paste your proofed text into Claude Code after this prompt:

Put my proofed text below into holzman-2026/brief-draft.md word for word. Keep its byline, headings, bold, its [Table 1 here] or [Figure 1 here] line and references. List every change to a number, a hedge or a causal verb.

- Check each listed change against the paper.

## 4. With author

The desk stops at Your proof. From here the folder is the record.

- Ask Claude Code: Run build-author-docx.js from erc-research-briefs on the holzman-2026 folder. If it says docx is missing, ask it to run npm install docx@9.7.1.

- It writes <folder>-author-review.docx into the brief's folder. Any gap shows in red as "Fix before sending: ..." on the page and in the terminal. Never send a copy with a red line.

- Email the Word copy to the author of record with a reply date. Add any number questions from step 3.

## 5. Approved

- Save the author's reply in the brief's folder under a new name, like holzman-2026-author-edits.docx. Under the old name, a rebuild would overwrite it. Never edit their file.

- Ask Claude Code:

Number every tracked change and comment in holzman-2026/holzman-2026-author-edits.docx. Mark each Accept or Push back, with the rule from erc-research-briefs/VOICE.md or REPORTING.md. Change no file yet.

- Decide each one yourself. Accept facts, corrected numbers, added hedges and plainer wording. Push back on claims firmer than the paper, jargon, "we" and a dropped null result.

- Tell Claude which numbered changes to apply. Then reread brief-draft.md for broken sentences.

- Reply to the author with a one-line reason for each change you did not make.

- Send a second round only if a finding, a number or the table moved. Rebuild the Word copy as in step 4.

- Approved means the author's yes in writing. Save that email in the folder as approved.pdf. In Outlook, choose File, Print, then PDF, Save as PDF. From then on the words are frozen.

## 6. HTML

Start only after the approval. Page breaks depend on the final words.

- Ask Claude Code:

Build holzman-2026/brief.html from a copy of erc-research-briefs/_template/brief-template.html. Never edit the template. Follow STANDARDIZATION.md and REPORTING.md in erc-research-briefs, with erc-research-briefs/bowen-2026/brief.html as the example. The visual is proposed-visual.md. Never cut or reword brief-draft.md to fit: give me options. Build a page at a time and say how far each runs over.

- Open brief.html in Chrome and press Cmd+P. Choose Save as PDF. Under More settings, choose Letter and Margins None, tick Background graphics and untick Headers and footers. Print while online so the fonts load.

- Send Claude one problem per message, then print again.

- A page cut off at the bottom, or white space under it: ask for the options. Words move between pages and are never cut.

- Em dashes: press Cmd+F and type Option+Shift+hyphen. Search for "[" too, for unfilled placeholders.

- A broken logo: its path should start ../erc-research-briefs/assets/.

## 7. Live

- Save the PDF in the brief's folder, named like Economics-of-Encouragement-ERC-RB-26.pdf.

- Open Drive in Chrome and share the PDF as Anyone with the link, Viewer. Copy the link.

- Add a row to ERC-Research-Briefs-Library-Metadata.xlsx in erc-research-briefs/, copying the rows above. Ask at the ERC who at the library takes it.

- On Submit content (https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/#team), paste the link and pick New research, then ERC Research. Type the brief's title in Title and the Executive Summary's first paragraph in Description. Press Add to the queue.

## Bring back the Google scripts

Two scripts, 15 minutes, any time.
 
Two Google scripts run inside two Sheets in the shared drive, under the login that set them up. When that account closes they stop until someone redeploys them. Nothing is lost.

Sheet | Where | What stops | 

erc-content-desk | ERC Content Desk, Content queue | The queue's Sheet copy, the scraper's files, the briefs' folders | 
 
subscribe to listserve | ERC Content Desk, Listserv | Listserv sign-ups | 

## For each Sheet

- Open it, then Extensions, Apps Script.
 
- Deploy, New deployment. Pick Web app from the gear.
 
- Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Deploy.
 
- Allow the permissions. If Google says unverified, choose Advanced, then Go to the project.
 
- Copy the Web app URL, ending in /exec, and email it to the desk's owner (the handoff document has the address). Say which Sheet.

## Check it

- Queue: a test item on the team form shows up in the Sheet within a minute. Delete it on Content Sort.
 
- Listserv: the Listserv page shows a number, not a warning.

## If it goes wrong

- "Unknown action" or a 500: send the newest Web app URL again.
 
- No Extensions menu: you need edit access to the Sheet.
 
- Leave the script's settings (Script properties) alone.
