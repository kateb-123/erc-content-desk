Design for the ERC

Claude Design, Canva and print.

Claude Design

  1. Open the Aggie Web Template link from the email once. It is Texas A&M's web design system.
  2. In Claude Design, say "use the Aggie Web Template design system".
  3. It is for web pages. For flyers and posts, use Canva and the values below.

Canva

Put these in a Brand Kit once (Brand, then Brand Kit).

ColorHEXUse
Aggie Maroon#500000Most of it
White#FFFFFFGround
Black#202020Body text
Dark Gray#3E3E3ESecond text
Gray#707070Captions, rules
Light Gray#D1D1D1Boxes
Light Maroon#732F2FRare accent

Fonts: Oswald for big titles in capitals, Work Sans for headings, Open Sans for body.

Logos: upload the ERC logo pack for screens. Never redraw, recolor or stretch one. Only on white or maroon.

Print

  • Use the ERC logo pack for print.
  • Maroon in CMYK is 15 100 39 69, Pantone 7421.
  • Body text 11 point or larger.
  • From Canva, download as PDF Print, with bleed if the printer asks.
