Design for the ERC

Claude Design, Canva and print, in the Texas A&M look.

In Claude Design

  1. Kate shares her design system with you: Aggie Web Template, Texas A&M's web design system (colors, fonts, buttons, icons, logos). Open the link from her email once.
  2. In Claude Design, start a design and say "use the Aggie Web Template design system". It then builds in maroon, Oswald, Work Sans and Open Sans.
  3. It is made for web pages. For a flyer or a social post, use the colors and fonts below.

In Canva

Set these once in a Brand Kit (Brand, then Brand Kit; it needs Canva Pro or Teams). Without it, save one design with them and copy it each time.

ColorHEXUse
Aggie Maroon#500000Most of any design: headlines, bands, buttons
White#FFFFFFThe ground
Black#202020Body text
Dark Gray#3E3E3ESecondary text
Gray#707070Captions, rules
Light Gray#D1D1D1Boxes, dividers
Light Maroon#732F2FAn accent, sparingly

Fonts (all in Canva): Oswald for big titles only, in capitals; Work Sans for headings and short text; Open Sans for body text, 11 point at least.

Logos: download the ERC logo pack for screens, ERC-logo-pack-RGB-for-web.zip, and upload the files to the Brand Kit. Never redraw, recolor or stretch a logo; put it only on white or maroon.

For print

  • Use the print logo pack, ERC-logo-pack-CMYK-for-print.zip.
  • Give the printer CMYK: Aggie Maroon 15 100 39 69 (Pantone 7421), Black 67 63 63 57, Dark Gray 68 61 60 48, Gray 19 12 13 34.
  • Body text 11 point or larger. Crimson Text is the print serif for research pieces.
  • In Canva, download print files as PDF Print, with crop marks and bleed when the printer asks.

Every time

  • Mostly maroon and white; gray to build order; accents rarely.
  • Text must read clearly against its background; never let color alone carry a meaning.
  • Every image gets alt text when it goes online.
