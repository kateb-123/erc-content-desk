# The team walkthrough: notes for Design

Brainstormed with Kate on Sep 23, 2026. Two pieces: one for the whole ERC team, one mainly for Kathy. Kate emails the team herself; this is the walkthrough the email points to, styled in Claude Design. Nothing here about the back of the desk (Content Sort, Finalize, Publish): that guide is forthcoming.

## Piece 1: the team

Who: everyone at the ERC who sends in content.

### Your page: Submit content

https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/#team (no password)

- The form. Paste a link and the desk fills in the title and description when it can. Pick a type, add your initials, Add to the queue. A fold under the form, Add a doc or spreadsheet, takes many at once.
- The Queue. What is waiting to be sorted, newest first. Read only.
- Quick links. The three public pages, each with Copy link.
- Two buttons. Newsletter (the hub, with the next issue's date under it) and Content Sort (Kate's, behind the password).

### What is public

Hand these out. Never the desk's own address.

- Submit content (the share form, for anyone outside the team): https://erc-share.vercel.app/submit/
- Join the listserv: https://erc-share.vercel.app/listserv/
- ERC Policy Exchange: https://erc-policy-exchange.vercel.app/

### Where a submission goes

- Everything is reviewed before it goes out.
- The newsletter goes out every other Tuesday. Next: Oct 6. In by 5pm Monday for Tuesday's issue.
- The Policy Exchange updates when Kate publishes.
- By kind:
  - An ERC event (hosted or co-hosted by the ERC) leads the newsletter under ERC Spotlight and goes to the Exchange under ERC Events.
  - Another unit's campus event (Event, A&M) goes to the newsletter only, unless Kate says otherwise.
  - Research, off-campus events, webinars, opportunities and headlines go to both.

### What helps

The desk asks for these. A missing one holds nothing up.

- The link, always.
- Event: date, time, place. A webinar needs no place.
- Research: authors, source, publication date.
- Opportunity: the deadline.
- Headline: source, date.
- A picture or a flyer if there is one. It rides into the newsletter and the Exchange, and the Exchange's front-page highlight needs one.

### The kinds the desk knows

- ERC event
- New Ed Policy Research: Working Paper, Peer-Reviewed, Report, ERC Research
- Event: A&M, Off-Campus, Webinar-Online
- Opportunity: Funding & Grants, Fellowships & Programs, Call for Proposals, Other
- Headline: National, Texas

## Piece 2: Kathy and the newsletter

Pages, no password on either:

- The Newsletter hub: https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/#newsletter (tabs: Next issue, Schedule, Past issues)
- The builder: https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/builder/

### Where the content comes from

1. The team submits (the page above, or the public share form).
2. Kate sorts on Content Sort and keeps an item with ERC Newsletter ticked where it belongs.
3. The item lands in the hub's Ready to add on Next issue. Add puts it in the issue; Quick add puts something straight in.
4. The builder pulls what the hub staged for the issue's date.

### The builder's four steps

1. Review: pick the issue, Pull from the desk. Pull again any time; only new items are added.
2. Outline: the sections and the items in order. Remove keeps the row greyed with Undo.
3. Preview & Edit: the email as it will look. Edit the words and the pictures.
4. Save & Export: save to the archive, download the HTML for sending.

### The email's sections, in order

Featured Research (Research Brief, Report), ERC Spotlight (Programs & Opportunities, Events, This & That), Upcoming Events (Featured Events, Texas A&M, Online & Off-Campus), Opportunities (Funding & Grants, Fellowships & Training, Calls for Proposals, Miscellaneous), New Education Policy Research (Working Papers, Peer-Reviewed, Miscellaneous), Education Headlines (Federal, Texas, Miscellaneous).

Past issues holds the archive, newest first, back to Nov 2025.
