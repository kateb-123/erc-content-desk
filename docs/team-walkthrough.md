Brainstormed with Kate on Sep 23, 2026, and checked against the desk's code on Sep 24. Two pieces: one for the whole ERC team, one mainly for Kathy. Kate emails the team herself. Nothing here about the back of the desk (Content Sort, Finalize, Publish): that guide is forthcoming.

Use the desk's own words for things: the names below are the ones on screen. Print no date that goes stale: the Newsletter page shows the next issue.

## Piece 1: the team

Who: everyone at the ERC who sends in content.

### Your page: Submit content

https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/#team (no password)

Left column:

- The form: Paste a link, Title, Description, the type, Your initials, Add to the queue.
  - The link and your initials are required. Title and description can stay blank: after Add to the queue the desk reads the link and fills in what it can.
  - The type can be left for Kate. Pick one and you pick its subtype too (every type but ERC event has them), or the form will not add it.
  - If the link is already waiting in the queue, the form says so and asks before adding it again.
  - Put the date, time and place, or the deadline, in the description.
  - The form takes no picture. The public share form does (PNG, JPG or PDF under 2.5 MB).
- Add a doc or spreadsheet, under the form: a .docx, .md, .txt, .xlsx or .csv with many items at once. Word and spreadsheet templates to fill in are there.
- Queue: what is waiting to be sorted, newest first. Read only. A new item shows once the desk has read its link; refresh if it is not there yet.

Right column:

- Quick links: the three public pages below, each with Copy link.
- Three buttons: Newsletter (the next issue's date under it), Content Sort and Policy Exchange. Content Sort and Policy Exchange are Kate's and ask for her password. The public Exchange is the Quick link.

### What is public

Hand these out. Never the desk's own address.

- Submit content, the share form for anyone outside the team: https://erc-share.vercel.app/submit/
- Join listserv: https://erc-share.vercel.app/listserv/
- ERC Policy Exchange: https://erc-policy-exchange.vercel.app/

### Where a submission goes

- Kate reviews everything before it goes out.
- The newsletter goes out every other Tuesday. In by 5pm Monday for Tuesday's issue.
- The Policy Exchange updates when Kate publishes.
- By type:
  - ERC event (hosted or co-hosted by the ERC): the newsletter's ERC Spotlight, and the Exchange tagged ERC Events.
  - Event, A&M (another unit's campus event): the newsletter only, unless Kate says otherwise.
  - Everything else: the newsletter and the Exchange.

### The types

- ERC event
- New research: Working Paper, Peer-Reviewed, Report, ERC Research
- Event: A&M, Off-Campus, Webinar-Online
- Opportunity: Funding & Grants, Fellowships & Programs, Call for Proposals, Other
- Headline: National, Texas

## Piece 2: Kathy and the newsletter

Who: Kathy builds the issue. Kate curates what goes in.

Pages, no password on either:

- Newsletter: https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/#newsletter (tabs: Next issue, Schedule, Past issues)
- The builder: https://erc-content-desk.vercel.app/builder/ (Open the builder, on Next issue)

### Where the content comes from

1. The team sends it in (Submit content, or the public share form), and Kate scrapes and curates.
2. Kate keeps an item on Content Sort. Every kept item goes to the newsletter. Her ERC Newsletter only box keeps an item off the Exchange, not off the newsletter.
3. Kept items wait in Ready to add on Next issue. Add puts one in the issue (In this issue). Quick add puts something straight in.
4. In the builder, Pull from the desk brings in what the issue holds for its date.

### The builder's four steps

1. Review: pick the issue, Pull from the desk. Pull again any time; only new items are added.
2. Outline: put the issue in order with the arrows, mark one event Featured, turn the research callout on or off. Remove keeps the row greyed with Undo. An item cannot move to another section here.
3. Preview & Edit: the email as it will look. This is for fixes: a typo, a title, a date, the introduction, a picture.
4. Save & Export: Copy HTML and paste the issue into Outlook, then Save to the archive. Download .html keeps a file copy.

Make every fix in Preview & Edit, before Copy HTML, so the archived issue matches what went out. Do not edit the email once it is in Outlook.

### The email's sections, in order

1. Featured Research: Research Brief, Report
2. ERC Spotlight: Programs & Opportunities, Events, This & That
3. Upcoming Events: Featured Events, Texas A&M, Online & Off-Campus
4. Opportunities: Funding & Grants, Fellowships & Training, Calls for Proposals, Miscellaneous
5. New Education Policy Research: Working Papers, Peer-Reviewed, Miscellaneous
6. Education Headlines: Federal, Texas
7. Miscellaneous

Past issues holds the archive, newest first, back to Nov 2025.
