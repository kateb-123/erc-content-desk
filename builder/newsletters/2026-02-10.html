Date: Monday, March 2nd | 3:30 - 5:30 PM
Location: Harrington 216
Learn More & RSVP

Please join us for our next PEP Talks! event, Lightning Talks in Educational Policy & Practice. Now Recruiting - ERC Graduate Student Ambassadors! 🧑‍🎓

A quick reminder that the ERC is still recruiting Graduate Student Ambassadors for a small, interdisciplinary group supporting ERC initiatives (~5 hours per week). If you meant to apply but have not yet, we would love to hear from you. This is a great way to get involved with education policy work, build relationships, and position yourself for future ERC research opportunities.

Carter-Larke Black History and Education Lecture

February 23, 2026  | 11:00am - 2:00pm
Memorial Student Center, Room 2300
Learn more and RSVP

The Carter-Larke Black History and Education Lecture honors the legacies of Dr. Norvella Carter and Dr. Patricia Larke, two trailblazing faculty whose scholarship and teaching transformed generations of educators. Since 2017, it has grown into a signature Black History Month event in the College of Education and Human Development. 

This year’s lecture, Nine Beats Strong: Rhythm, Resistance, and Renaissance, offers an engaging program exploring Black history, culture, and education through the lens of rhythm and resistance. Hear from keynote speaker Dr. Tony B. Watlington Sr., superintendent of the School District of Philadelphia, and join a campus-wide conversation on educational justice and renewal.

PEER Center Summer Scholars Program  

Dates: June 3-5
Location: Washington, D.C.
Application Deadline: March 1st, 2026
Learn more here

The Postsecondary Education & Economics Research (PEER) Center invites early- and mid-career academics conducting higher education policy research to apply for PEER Summer Scholars, a selective training and networking opportunity in Washington, D.C.

Over three days, participants will learn how the federal policymaking process works from experts with government experience, strengthen skills for communicating research to broader audiences, and connect with journalists and policymakers in Congress and the Executive Branch. Scholars will work closely with PEER staff and have opportunities to engage directly with senior academics, media, and policy leaders.

Olympics Trivia
The 2026 Winter Olympics kicked off Friday in Italy. This marks the first time in Olympic history that two host cities in the same country are co-hosting the Games: Milan and Cortina.

Trivia Questions: 
1. Excluding this year, which countries hosted the last 3 winter Olympics?

2. For decades, the Winter and Summer Games took place in the same year. Which were the first Winter Olympics under the new staggered schedule still in use today?

3. How many medals (gold, silver, and bronze) are up for grabs across the Winter Olympics?
