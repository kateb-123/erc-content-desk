Howdy Aggies! March is around the corner, and so are a bunch of great chances to learn, share work, and meet people across campus. Here’s what’s coming up at ERC and across Texas A&M this week—events, opportunities, and ways to connect with colleagues

ERC Spotlight

This week’s ERC spotlight is our next PEP Talks! event, Lightning Talks in Educational Policy & Practice, followed immediately by ERC Happy Hour. If you’re looking for a low-lift way to learn what other Aggies are working on, offer quick feedback, and meet people across departments, this is the perfect drop-in!

Lightning Talks in Educational Policy & Practice (PEP Talks!) ⚡

Date: Monday, March 2nd | 3:30 - 5:30 PM
Location: Harrington 216
Learn More & RSVP

Please join us for our next PEP Talks! event, Lightning Talks in Educational Policy & Practice.

    Esteban Aucejo, Arizona State University

Voices of Impact Speaker Series - 10th Anniversary

February 26, 2026  | 6:00
Memorial Student Center, Bethancourt Ballroom
Learn More and RSVP

Voices of Impact celebrates its 10th anniversary this year, bringing together 12 faculty members to share their research in clear, accessible talks. In five-minute bursts, speakers will unpack issues ranging from AI and digital literacy to mental health, special education, and teacher wellness. Join us for an evening of good food, big ideas, and stories about how College of Education and Human Development researchers are working to be a force for good in our communities and beyond.
 

CEHD Speaker Series - Dr. Matthew Kraft

March 3rd, 20216  | 9:30 - 11:00am
Memorial Student Center, 2406B
Learn More

Join Dr. Matthew Kraft (Brown University) for a CEHD Speaker Series talk on whether teacher effectiveness carries across very different school contexts. Drawing on evidence from the Talent Transfer Initiative and a randomized $20,000 transfer incentive, Kraft examines how high-performing teachers’ effectiveness changes after moving to low-achieving schools, and what drives those shifts.
 

2026 GRAB Symposium

The Graduate Representative Advisory Board is excited to present the 2026 GRAB Symposium. Join us and our partners to celebrate graduate student research. All students, faculty, and staff in the College of Education and Human Development are encouraged to attend. Lunch will be provided. 

Keynote Speakers: Drs.  Mary Margaret Capraro and Robert Capraro
 

Beyond Texas A&M Events

NASBE Webinar - Leveraging Apprenticeships to Rebuild the Educator Workforce

March 3rd, 2026 | 2:00pm - 3:00pm
Virtual
Learn More and RSVP

Join NASBE for a discussion on how state boards of education can expand and strengthen the educator workforce through new, flexible pathways. Delaware education leaders will share how their state is driving post-pandemic workforce growth through a stackable apprenticeship model, including registered paraprofessional and teacher apprenticeships that lead to a seamless, debt-free pathway and a year-long teacher residency.
