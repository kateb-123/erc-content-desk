Research Brief

Can Intensive Advising Open Doors to Selective Colleges?
Evidence from the EMERGE Fellowship

Brian Holzman, Ph.D., Irina Chukhray, Ph.D., Courtney Thrash

High-achieving, low-income students apply to selective colleges far less often than wealthier peers, even with similar academic records. Can the right kind of advising close that gap? A new brief examines the EMERGE Fellowship, a multi-year college-access program in a large Texas school district, and finds that participants were 17 percentage points more likely to apply to selective colleges, with gains driven by advising rather than higher test scores.
