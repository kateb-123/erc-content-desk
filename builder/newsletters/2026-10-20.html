Eminent Scholar Lecture Series - Dr. Bob Pianta

October 27, 2026 | 4:00 PM CT | Rudder Auditorium Complex, Forum, College Station, TX

The talk covers his work on teaching quality, early childhood education, and the systems that support student success.
