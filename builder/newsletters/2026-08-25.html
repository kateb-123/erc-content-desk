ERC Research

At the ERC, our mission is to transform education policy and practice by bridging the gap between rigorous academic research and real-world impact. Whether we’re publishing in top-tier journals, breaking down key legislation through ERC Explains, or translating findings into concise briefs for policymakers and practitioners, we strive to make complex data accessible.
