ERC Research

Research Report

The Pipeline of Alternative Teacher Certification: Evaluating iteach

Andrew Kwok, Brendan Bartanen, Kate Barnes

Alternative certification now supplies most new Texas teachers, but evidence on how those teachers fare has been limited. This ERC study tracks a decade of iteach candidates in Texas, Virginia, and Louisiana, following who applies, enrolls, finishes, and stays teaching. The pipeline is expanding rapidly; Texas applications have tripled since 2015. Many join mid-career from other fields. Notably, five years in, iteach completers remain in classrooms at higher rates than their peers.
