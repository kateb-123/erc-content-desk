RESEARCH SUPPORT SERVICES

 

Texas A&M’s Education Research Center provides specialized consulting services to support faculty, students, and research teams across the university. We can partner with you on everything from early project scoping and methodological design to data acquisition, advanced statistical analysis, and report generation.


Whether you are exploring a new idea, planning an evaluation, or preparing to launch a full study, we are here to help! For projects that may require access to statewide education or workforce data, we can also advise on available data resources and guide you through the appropriate request processes as needed. To explore the types of statewide data commonly used in ERC-supported projects, click here!


If you have a potential project or are simply considering one and would like support, please fill out the short form below so we can learn more about your needs and connect you with our team.

CALLS FOR PROPOSALS & FUNDING OPPORTUNITIES

 

Below are several active calls for proposals and funding opportunities that may be relevant to your work. We encourage you to explore and share these opportunities with colleagues!

CALL FOR CHAPTERS

 

Advancing the Educative Power of Student Belonging in Higher Education: Voice from College Classrooms

Submit a chapter proposal for the forthcoming edited volume Advancing the Educative Power of Student Belonging in Higher Education: Voices from College Classrooms. The book highlights innovative, research-driven approaches for fostering student belonging across postsecondary contexts, including in-person, hybrid, and online learning environments.

Topics may include:

  • Frameworks for student belonging and wellness

  • Pedagogical practices that support belonging

  • Faculty–student relationship building

  • Empirical studies of belonging interventions

  • Institutional and classroom-level strategies

ℹ️ Click here for more information

CALL FOR RESEARCH

 

The Common App

Common App invites researchers to propose studies using its extensive de-identified data warehouse, with an emphasis this year on high school course-taking data. Projects should yield actionable insights related to college preparedness, course-taking patterns, academic opportunity, or student skill development.

ℹ️ Click here for more information

SPECIAL PROJECT GRANTS (FY 2025)

 

U.S. Department of Education: Fund for the Improvement of Postsecondary Education – Special Projects (FIPSE-SP)

The Department of Education is accepting applications for the FY 2025 Fund for the Improvement of Postsecondary Education – Special Projects (FIPSE-SP) program. Grants support innovative projects addressing one or more of four national areas of need:

  • Advancing AI in Postsecondary Education

  • Promoting Civil Discourse on College Campuses

  • Accreditation Reform

  • Capacity-Building for High-Quality Short-Term Programs
