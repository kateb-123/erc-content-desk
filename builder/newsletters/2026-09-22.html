Resource Spotlight: Federal Data at Texas A&M

Thank you to Dr. Mary Campbell, Director of the Texas Research Data Center (TXRDC), for joining us for our first talk of the semester.

The TXRDC works with the U.S. Census Bureau to host a secure lab at Texas A&M where approved researchers can use restricted-access federal data. For education researchers, that means versions of the American Community Survey, Current Population Survey, and decennial census with larger samples, finer geography, more detailed variables, and links between datasets that public files don't allow. Texas A&M leads the TXRDC Consortium, so our researchers can run funded projects at reduced lab fees, and in many cases the fees are waived.

Curious whether your project is a fit? Dr. Campbell and Dr. Karin Johnson will cover what data are available and how to apply for access at an informational session this Thursday. Details below under Upcoming Events.
