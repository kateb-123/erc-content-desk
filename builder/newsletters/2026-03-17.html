Aggies at AEFP!

The 2026 AEFP Annual Conference takes place in Chicago this week (March 19–21), and the Aggie presence is hard to miss. AEFP, the Association for Education Finance and Policy,  is an annual gathering for education policy researchers, practitioners, and policymakers.

Texas A&M researchers and affiliates are presenting this year, with work spanning COVID-19 and student outcomes, arts education, teacher residencies, community college credentials, and more. Even if you're not in Chicago, it's a good reminder of the breadth of education policy work happening right here at A&M, and a great reason to cheer on your colleagues from afar!

SRCD Future of the U.S. Policy Fellowship Task Force

The Society for Research in Child Development (SRCD) is seeking individuals to join the Future of the U.S. Policy Fellowship Task Force — a one-year commitment to help refresh and strengthen one of the nation's longest-standing research-policy fellowships. Task Force members will revisit the Fellowship's purpose, recommend changes to program design, support new funding efforts, and help shape the Fellowship's future direction.
