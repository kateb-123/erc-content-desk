Introducing the ERC Policy Exchange

One place to keep up with education policy. The ERC now curates a running feed of new working papers and studies, upcoming webinars and conferences, open grants and fellowships, and the Texas and national headlines we’re following. Filter by type, sort by deadline, and see what’s closing soon. Updated regularly at the ERC Policy Exchange.
 

Dr. Jee Han Receives a Spencer Foundation Grant

Steering committee member Dr. Jee Han has won a Spencer Foundation research grant for “Administrative Discretion in Grade Retention and Its Effects on Longer-term Student Outcomes,” a project built on Texas ERC data. Congratulations, Dr. Han!
