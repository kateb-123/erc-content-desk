ERC Graduate Research Program - Summer 2026

🗣️🗣️ Calling faculty and graduate students! 🗣️🗣️

The Education Research Center is launching its Summer 2026 Graduate Research Program, pairing doctoral students with faculty who need additional research support. The program focuses on authentic data collection and analysis, with the goal of producing scholarly dissemination products.

This is a flexible, summer-only collaboration — designed to supplement, not replace, existing assistantships. Faculty and students both apply separately, and matching is shaped by student preferences gathered after faculty present their projects.

For faculty: Apply by April 15th and plan to present your project at the ERC "In the Works: Faculty Research" Talk on April 23rd so students can view and rank preferences.

For students: Apply by April 15th via the Google form.

Deadline: April 15, 2026 
Contact: akwok@tamu.edu

Texas A&M Events

Literacy Studies Group Seminar — The New Definition of Dyslexia

Date & Time: Monday, April 6 | 11:00am–12:00pm
Location: Zoom
Click here for more information

Ph.D. candidate Juan Quinonez Beltran (TLAC, Texas A&M) presents "The Path to the Revised Definition of Dyslexia," walking through the multi-phase process the International Dyslexia Association used to update its 2002 definition. Each session includes a 45-minute presentation followed by Q&A.
 

IPEG Talk - Political Economy of School Desegregation

Date & Time: Thursday, April 16 | 4:15pm
Location: Allen 3072

Cody Tuttle (UT Austin) presents "A Different World: Enduring Effects of School Desegregation on Ideology and Attitudes," using quasi-random variation from a 1975 federal busing order in Jefferson County, KY, to examine long-run effects on political views and social attitudes. Part of the Interdepartmental Political Economy Group Seminar Series.

EdFund 2026 Request for Proposals

EdFund funds research projects that generate evidence to inform school finance policy. Their 2026 RFP has been updated: multiple submissions per institution are accepted (each as a separate Letter of Interest), and an informational webinar is scheduled for Tuesday, April 7, at 12:30pm ET for Q&A on funding interests.

Key Details: Info webinar April 7, 2026 | 12:30pm ET |  Full RFP → ed-fund.org

Spencer Foundation Small Research Grant

Supports education research projects up to $50,000 for 1–5 years. Applications are accepted twice per year — a great option for smaller-scale studies.

Key Details: Deadline April 15, 2026 | Learn more

NNERRP Annual Forum 2026 - Call for Session Ideas

The NNERPP Annual Forum (July 22–24, Los Angeles) is celebrating 10 years of NNERPP — and this year, they're doing something different. Rather than just collecting session proposals, they're inviting ideas from anyone — members or not, attending or not — to help shape the Forum agenda and year-round learning agenda. Submit ideas about RPP questions, challenges, and successes.
