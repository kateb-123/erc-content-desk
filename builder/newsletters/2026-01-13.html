Upcoming Events

Below are several upcoming events and workshops, both in-person and virtual, that may be of interest to our ERC community.
 
 

Families at the Center: Shaping the Future of Education

January 20, 2026
12:30–2:00 pm ET (Virtual)
Registration Link

Hosted by the Campaign for Grade-Level Reading, this virtual event focuses on the role of families and communities in children’s early school success. Speakers from Learning Heroes and Brookings will share new research on family engagement and school attendance, then philanthropic leaders will discuss how they are using these insights to guide their work with practitioners.
 

Measuring School Quality: What Multiple Measures Reveal about Students

January 28, 2026
3:00 - 4:00pm ET (Virtual)
Registration Link

Join the National Association of State Boards of Education (NASBE) for a conversation on how to better measure school quality. Researchers from MIT Blueprint Labs will share new findings on how student surveys and test scores align and how each relates to long-term student success.

Funding Opportunity:

Charter School Research Collaborative

The Charter School Research Collaborative at MIT’s Blueprint Labs has opened its fifth request for proposals to support policy‑relevant research aligned with its charter school agenda. Grants range from 10,000 to $500,000, and mandatory letters of inquiry are due February 5, 2026.  

To date, the Collaborative has funded 30 projects across a range of topics and states. Prospective applicants are encouraged to attend an informational webinar or office hours to learn more about fit and expectations.

Learn more here
 

Call for Chapters:

A Good IDEA: School–University–Community Collaboration to Enhance Learning for Students with Disabilities

This edited volume marks the 50th anniversary of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and invites chapters that highlight effective strategies for expanding educational opportunities for students with learning disabilities beyond traditional school-based services.

Submission Deadline Date: March 30, 2026
